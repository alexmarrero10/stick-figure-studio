# Changelog

The version lives in the `VERSION` file at the repo root. It shows next to the app name and in
`/api/settings/health`. Numbers follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH: bump PATCH for fixes, MINOR for new features,
MAJOR for big changes (for example a new database layout that old projects can't open).

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-06)

First numbered version. Everything from idea to finished video:
- Ideas, research and script (with RAG research from 10-15 downloaded sources when set up), beats,
  ElevenLabs voice (speed control, pause trimming), image prompts for Google Flow, image import,
  assembly of the video and the vertical Short, and the publishing pack.
- Your video site: publish videos to Supabase for the public site (search plus a chatbot with
  timestamps and guard rails).
- Evals that score the RAG search, the chatbot's answers, its guard rails, and RAG's speed and token use.
